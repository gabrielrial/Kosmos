## STAR DETECTOR
### WHITE THRESHOLD
The value is represented in HSV
hue, saturation, and value (brightness)
Hue (H): The color family or type. It is measured in degrees from 0 to 360. Red is at 0°, green is at 120°, and blue is at 240°.Saturation (S): How pure or strong the color is. It goes from 0% (gray) to 100% (full, rich color).Value (V): How bright the color is. It goes from 0% (pitch black) to 100% (full light).

In our case we will only use Saturation a Value.
Both values go from 0.0 to 1.0, where 0 is 0% and 1 means 100%

The key of this white threshold is do define how bright a pixel should be to be potencially consider as a star.
